About This Tool

This open-source calculator implements the 14-criterion CO2 geological storage screening framework developed for Paper 1 of Daniel T. Olagunju's doctoral research at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS. The framework was validated against 16 global saline aquifer formations spanning North America, Europe, Australia, and Asia.

All 10 physics criteria (C1–C10) are computed analytically from closed-form equations. No machine-learning surrogates. No black boxes. C11–C14 are regulatory permit checklists aligned with EPA UIC Class VI and equivalent frameworks.

Criterion Summary

  • C1 Mohr-Coulomb fault reactivation
  • C2 Caprock seal integrity (Winland r35)
  • C3 Area of Review (Cooper-Jacob)
  • C4 Convective dissolution (Rayleigh-Darcy)
  • C5 Halite precipitation (Zeidouni 2009)
  • C6 Supercritical phase (Span-Wagner)
  • C7 Capillary seal overpressure
  • C8 Volumetric storage capacity
  • C9 Injectivity index (Darcy radial)
  • C10 Monitoring feasibility (plume)
  • C11 USDW exemption
  • C12 Legacy well assessment
  • C13 Pore-space rights
  • C14 EIA complete
